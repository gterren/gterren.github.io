Data and models

GridPath-India

Capacity expansion and production cost model of India's electricity system, built on the open-source GridPath platform. 34 load zones plus Bhutan, interstate transmission, and hourly demand from linearly scaled and bottom-up profiles, with investment and operations optimized across planning periods from 2020 to 2050. Includes more than 1,300 candidate wind and solar sites and project-level capacity factors.

Dryad · 10.5061/dryad.dz08kpsbm

Girasol

A sky imaging and global solar irradiance dataset. Longwave infrared and multi-exposure visible-light sky images with matching pyranometer measurements, recorded every 15 seconds by the Girasol Machine on a solar tracker, for intra-hour solar forecasting.
